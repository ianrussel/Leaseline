How it works

TICKET 01

Forward it in

Paste a message, forward the email, or connect your inbox - whatever's already landing gets routed to Faultline as-is.

TICKET 02

Triaged in ~2 seconds

Category, urgency, and a drafted reply come back - even for messages that bundle two or three issues into one rambling text.

TICKET 03

You approve, it's sent

One click to send the draft as-is, edit it first, or escalate straight to your vendor. Nothing goes out without you.

Never miss the real emergency

Gas smells, flooding, no heat in winter - flagged Emergency or High automatically, even buried in a rambling message.

One clean reply, every time

Consistent, professional tone on every response. Edit in one click - no blank page staring back at you at 11pm.

Splits multi-issue messages

One text about the sink and the hallway light becomes two tickets, each triaged and routed on its own.
