🚀 Big news — I'm building something new!

For the past few weeks I've been heads-down on Faultline, an AI-powered
maintenance triage assistant for small property managers and landlords.

Here's the problem it solves: property managers get flooded with tenant
texts and emails about maintenance stuff — a leak, a broken light, a gas
smell — and have to figure out by hand what's actually urgent and reply
to each one. Faultline reads the message the moment it lands, tags what's
wrong and how urgent it is, and hands back a ready-to-send reply — all in
about 2 seconds. (Check out the short clip below to see it in action.)

Under the hood, I built a custom training dataset and I'm fine-tuning an
open-source AI model on it (Hugging Face + QLoRA) — so it's not just a
generic ChatGPT wrapper, it's actually trained for this one job.

A bit about me: I'm a self-taught full-stack developer — started at 34,
now working in AI model fine-tuning — and this is my first real shot at
turning that into a product people can use day to day.

It's in beta right now — free to join, no credit card needed. If you're
a landlord, property manager, or know someone who is, I'd love to get
them in and hear what they think!
